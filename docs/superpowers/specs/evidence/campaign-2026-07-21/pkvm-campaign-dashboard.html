Where the coverage lands (target)

Of ~6,407 total PCs, most are the executor's own sandbox setup (lib/mm/fs). The target slice is arch/arm64/kvm — ~597 PCs:

The pKVM lifecycle functions the corpus reaches — the whole create→run→destroy path. These are host (EL1) functions in arch/arm64/kvm/pkvm.c — the host's driver for hyp VMs. "hyp" is what they manage, not where they run.

Full host-side lifecycle covered: pkvm_init_host_vm → pkvm_create_hyp_vm → __pkvm_create_hyp_vm → __pkvm_create_hyp_vcpu, teardown __pkvm_destroy_hyp_vm → pkvm_unmap_range.
Coverage stops at the EL1→EL2 hypercall (kvm_call_hyp_nvhe(__pkvm_init_vm)). The EL2 image (__kvm_nvhe_*) has 0 sancov — that's dark, and it's Stage 2.
