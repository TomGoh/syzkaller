WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
    KVM_RUN still returns -EINVAL for a SUSPENDED vCPU. That is unchanged and
    correct: EL2 accepts only RUNNABLE and STOPPED, and this fix is about the
    cleanup on that rejection, not about accepting the state. Anyone re-running
    this probe should expect the -EINVAL and check dmesg, not the exit code.

    The three adjacent leaks that ACK 2b4d43af6 also fixes (hyp-pool SVE
    allocation on pkvm_vcpu_init_psci() failure; unpinning never-pinned SVE
    pages; pvmfw_entry_vcpu left dangling) are NOT addressed by this change and
    NOT tested here. Two are unreachable on N90, which has no SVE. They remain
    open.

ALSO IN THIS KERNEL, PREVIOUSLY UNTESTED ON HARDWARE
    #18 is the first deployed kernel to carry c6c3a2503e4b (account hyp
    donations even when the topup fails) and 22f8b90f89c7 (propagate the
    dirty-log granule probe's own error). Both were committed after #16 was
    built and verified, in response to review, and had never run on a board.
    So #18 changes four things relative to what was last verified, not one.
